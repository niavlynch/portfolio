About the trek

I have been chosen alongside my co-leader Thomas to be challenge leaders for ATU to take on Kilimanjaro in 2027. As challenge leader our role is to recruit team members, of which we have recruited 12 so far in ATU, to help our team members organise fundraising events to meet their fundraising goals and to help our team members train and prepare for the 9-day hike. We are on a trajectory to raise tens of thousands of euros as a team. The challenge has only begun so I will soon be adding more information and photos to this page come 2027. I have designed the logo and Thomas and I equally run the social media pages.
