Eye Cinema Branding

Graphic Design · Loyalty programme branding and promotional graphics for Eye Cinema · Tools used: Adobe Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, Procreate

About the project

I worked in the Eye Cinema, Galway as a Projectionist and as their Graphic Designer for around 2 years. This branding campaign was one I did during the building of their new website. It is a rebrand that kept Eye Cinema's iconic blue colour palette and the illustrations I made were designed to be usable again and again by the cinema, and are still in use to this day, in person at their concessions desk, during the adverts before screenings, on their social media and on their website. This one project I did proved to be incredibly useful to Eye Cinema and will continue to be for years to come.
