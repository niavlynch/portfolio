Charity Pub Quiz Nights

Personal pursuit · Self-hosted charity pub quiz nights

About the quiz

I am highly interested in being involved and invested in my local community, as well as fundraising for charity, so I have been hosting pub quizzes in The Ol’ 55 bar this year, with my third edition being hosted this December! The pub quizzes are organised, hosted and advertised by myself, but they would not be possible without Galway’s fantastic community who attend, and my wonderful friends who help with all the correcting!
